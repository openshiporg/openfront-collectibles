# Storefront (customer) surface brief

Scope: every customer route under `/[countryCode]`: home, store, categories, collections, product, search, cart, checkout, order confirmation, account (all sections), trade-in. Mode: Persuade on home; Operate everywhere else. Audience and job are in PRODUCT.md. Constraints: every route, state and workflow stays; never drop products, categories or collections; the owner pinned a dark, premium aesthetic (2026-10-24 brief).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The storefront is a collector's black-page stock book. Each specimen sits in a clear glassine strip on a black page, the categories are the cover's thumb index, and the store's name is stamped on buckram cloth. It refuses the cream auction-catalogue page (the previous look) and the gradient-glow dark shop.
OWN-WORLD: Black album page (oklch 14.5%) with paper-white ink. Buckram cover cloth in the store's logo hue carries the header band, the home cover, the slab band and the footer, and gilt from the same hue is the one accent: primary action, chosen pill, hover. Rokkitt slab sets stamped labels, numbers, prices and display; Literata sets everything read. Corners are square, at 2px.
STORY: The collector sees the store's own title on its cover, picks a category from the thumb index or browses everything, reads each specimen through its pocket and entry, opens the record (condition, grade, provenance, valuation), and buys or trades in.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width cloth cover panel. On the left, the store title stamped very large (up to 104px) with its description, Browse all items and Trade in. On the right edge, the category thumb-index tabs as filled bars that slide in on hover.
FORM: Black-page stock book, number 7 on my ordered list (1 evening sale catalogue, 2 museum vitrine, 3 coin-cabinet tray, 4 safe-deposit wall, 5 certificate of authenticity, 6 nine-pocket binder, 7 dealer's stock book). seed:9ff9359178aad073. Raises: every figure stamped to one column grid (cassette J-card); each entry shows only the dates and history its record holds (memory quilt). Signature interaction: the specimen lifts inside its glassine pocket on hover, and the pocket clears.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
